## Image audit

- All nine selected JPG URLs responded successfully when checked. None repeats a photograph from products 01-12 or another entry in this file.
- Alternate views of the same garment were intentionally omitted, including the navy formal suit, the floral evening jacket, the white women's suit and the floral wrap dress.
- The light blue and pale lilac rail photographs are too visually similar to justify two separate product listings; only the light blue photograph is listed.
- Showroom views, consultations, fabric books, close-ups without a clear full product, graphics and photographs bearing another shirtmaker's label were not converted into additional product listings.
- Product categories are suggested names. If a category already exists in your Business Profile, choose that existing category rather than creating a near-duplicate.
