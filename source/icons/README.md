# First-party interface icons

These small SVGs replace the per-page requests to Iconify and Simple Icons. `manifest.json` preserves each original source URL and its local path. Their colours and shapes are unchanged; the S.T Tailor logo is a separate original asset.

- Lucide icons: [project](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), [ISC/MIT license](LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt).
- Social brand symbols: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), [CC0 project license](SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md) and [brand-specific disclaimer](SIMPLE-ICONS-DISCLAIMER.md). A brand mark still belongs to its owner; its use here identifies an outbound profile.

When changing a symbol, update its SVG and the manifest together. Built pages must not request icons from those third-party CDNs.
